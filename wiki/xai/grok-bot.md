## Access and limits

- Grok Bot can be used from **desktop and iOS**. The only desktop download currently named on the official page is **macOS on Apple silicon**.
- Its official pricing page names six currently eligible tiers: Cursor Pro+, Cursor Ultra, SuperGrok Plus, SuperGrok Heavy, Cursor Teams Standard, and Cursor Teams Premium.
- The official page confirms only that eligible plans include **weekly Grok Bot usage**. It does not state the weekly count, reset time, token-to-usage conversion, per-task allowance, concurrent-Bot cap, or a paid overage mechanism.
- The price column records only values displayed on the official Grok Bot page. It must not be read as the price of every tier in a plan group.
