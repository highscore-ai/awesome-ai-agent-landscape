## Subscription access

| Access | Published usage behavior |
|---|---|
| Free tier | Chat and Voice have separate free-tier limits. |
| Paid Grok plan | Includes a weekly usage allowance for paid features. |
| SuperGrok | Higher model access and early features; plan-specific weekly allowance. |
| Extra Usage Credits | Available after the weekly paid-feature limit is reached. |
| Auto Top Up | Available for continued paid use. |

Grok does not publish a single fixed public task/token quota for paid subscriptions. Weekly usage can differ by plan and feature. When the weekly allowance is exhausted, paid features pause until reset while free Chat and Voice limits remain separate.
