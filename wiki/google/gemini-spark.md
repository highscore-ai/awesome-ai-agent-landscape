| Plan | Price shown | Gemini Spark access |
|---|---:|---|
| Free | US$0 | Not included |
| Google AI Plus | US$4.99/month | Not listed as included |
| Google AI Pro | US$19.99/month | Included in the current rollout |
| Google AI Ultra — 5× | Starts at US$99.99/month | Included in the current rollout |
| Google AI Ultra — 20× | US$199.99/month | Included in the current rollout |

Spark is available to Pro and Ultra subscribers in English. Availability is rolling out by country; it is not an API product and Google does not publish a fixed task, token, or credit quota for Spark.
