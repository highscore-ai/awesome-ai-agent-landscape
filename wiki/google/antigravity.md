# Google Antigravity

[Official Antigravity pricing](https://antigravity.google/pricing/)

> Verified 25 August 2026.

| Access tier | Price | Published access |
|---|---:|---|
| Individual | US$0/month | Gemini 3.5 Flash, Gemini 3.1 Pro, Gemini 3 Flash, Claude Sonnet & Opus 4.6, and gpt-oss-120b; unlimited Tab completions and Command requests; basic weekly agent-model limits |
| Google AI Pro | Google AI Pro plan | Entry agent-model rate limits |
| Google AI Ultra | US$100 or US$200/month | Higher agent-model rate limits; 5× or 20× capacity versus AI Pro |
| Gemini Enterprise | Contract subscription | Antigravity is included for eligible subscriptions |

Antigravity 2.0 is a standalone desktop application available on macOS, Linux, and Windows. Exact weekly rate-limit numbers are not published publicly.
