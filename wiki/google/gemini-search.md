# Gemini

[Official Gemini app](https://gemini.google/)

> Consumer plan prices in USD where shown. Verified 25 August 2026.

| Plan | Price shown | Gemini app usage |
|---|---:|---|
| Free | US$0 | Baseline access |
| Google AI Plus | US$4.99/month | 2× higher Gemini usage than Free |
| Google AI Pro | US$19.99/month | 4× higher Gemini usage than Free; higher access to Gemini 3 Pro, Deep Search, and agentic features |
| Google AI Ultra — 5× | Starts at US$99.99/month | 5× higher Gemini usage than AI Pro |
| Google AI Ultra — 20× | US$199.99/month | 20× higher Gemini usage than AI Pro |

Plans and availability vary by country. The Gemini app has separate product limits; a Gemini subscription does not create Gemini Developer API credits.
